# Gamebox 10 🎮📸

**10 mini-games and a camera in one lightweight Android app.**

Gamebox 10 includes Match 3, Memory Cards, 2048, Block Puzzle, Endless Runner, Tic Tac Toe, Snake, Word Puzzle, Reaction Tap, and Number Guess, plus a camera shortcut.

## How credits work

- New installations begin with **3 free credits**.
- Starting a new game session costs **1 credit**; restarting within that session is free.
- A successfully saved camera photo costs **1 credit**.
- Completing an available rewarded advertisement grants **3 credits**. Ad availability and serving depend on approval and location.

## Compatibility and photos

Android 7.0 or newer. Camera photos are saved to app storage; on Android 10+ the app also attempts to export them to **Pictures/Gamebox10** in the Gallery. App-stored photos may be removed if you uninstall the app.
