# Gamebox 10 — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 11 October 2026**

Gamebox 10 is an Android app featuring mini-games, a camera shortcut, and optional rewarded advertisements.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores your credit balance locally using Android SharedPreferences. It creates photos in app-specific storage when you use the camera. On Android 10 or newer, it also attempts to copy captured photos into the Pictures/Gamebox10 folder in your device's media library.

## Camera

The app launches your device's camera application to capture photos. Photos are stored on your device. Gamebox 10 does not intentionally upload captured photos to our servers.

## Advertising

Gamebox 10 includes the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)**. If advertisements are enabled and available, Google and its partners may collect or process device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interaction information, diagnostics, and other data for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention, subject to Google's policies and applicable consent requirements.

Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Internet access

The app may connect to the internet to request advertising content. Gameplay and local photo storage do not require an account with us.

## Sharing and retention

We do not operate an app backend for user accounts or uploaded photos. Local data remains on your device until deleted through app/device controls or uninstall. Advertising data processing and retention are governed by Google and its partners.

## Children

The app has not been specifically designed or assessed for children. Advertising and consent configurations must be reviewed before a public release directed at children.

## Your choices

You can decline optional rewarded ads, manage advertising privacy choices through your device settings, and remove app data by clearing storage or uninstalling the app. Photos exported to the Gallery may remain after uninstalling.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/josephebinezar2-rgb/Gamebox10-Releases/issues (avoid posting sensitive personal information publicly).

_This policy should be reviewed and updated with a dedicated privacy contact and applicable consent disclosures before public distribution._
